The Lock Screen between recordings

During a recording the Lock Screen shows the Live Activity from page 3. Between recordings it shows two small widgets under the clock and one line above it. They replace today's permanent “Recorder connected” banner.

Why widgets and not the banner

  • A widget stays for good. The banner is a Live Activity, which iOS ends after eight hours and only lets the app restart when it is opened.
  • It leaves the banner free to mean one thing: a recording is happening or has just finished.
  • It is your choice to place them; the app works the same without.

What it costs

  • Widgets update on a budget iOS controls, so “out of reach” can appear a few minutes late. The banner updates at once.
  • Lock Screen widgets are one colour and very small: one fact each.
  • They are a new piece to build: a widget needs its own shared storage with the app.

If you would rather keep the banner

It can stay as an option: “Show the link on the Lock Screen”. It would look like today's, in the Logbook style, and turn into the recording banner when a note starts. The eight-hour limit remains, so after a night it is gone until the app is opened once.
